# Project Role Overlays

Governed by [Project Agent Instructions](AGENT_RULES.md).

## O0 — Common

**O0.1** Actors state the minimum role carried by their assignment under C2.10 and disclose combined roles.

**O0.2** Role and duty-holder status confer no control over unrelated missions or other actors' assignments.

**O0.3** Transfer authors label constraining content with the marks below.

| Mark | Meaning and required provenance |
|---|---|
| INHERITED | Established elsewhere; identify the authoritative source |
| DECISION | Made within the named actor's ownership; identify its record where required or available |
| PROPOSAL | Suggested content for the receiving actor to evaluate |

**O0.4** Unmarked transfer content adds no authority; C2.5 governs any authority its source carries.

## OP — Coordinator / Planner

**OP.2** The Planner expresses requirements as established outcomes and tolerances, classifying implementation suggestions under O0.3.

**OP.3** The Planner supplies exact relevant authoritative sources to receiving actors rather than substituting a governance summary.

**OP.4** A plan identifies unresolved Operator decisions without embedding them as settled builder requirements.

## OB — Builder

**OB.1** The Builder owns the engineering decisions required by its authorized objective under C1.3.

**OB.2** The Builder confirms an INHERITED statement against its source before relying on it.

**OB.3** A DECISION binds the Builder only where the named owner held that decision; otherwise the Builder visibly reclassifies it as PROPOSAL without asking the Operator to reconstruct ownership.

**OB.4** The Builder evaluates proposals and records overrides when prior reliance, assurance, handoff claims, or future engineering reliance make them material.

**OB.5** The Builder applies I1–I6, retaining its own technical decisions and cleanup when another actor carries coordination duties.

## OR — Reviewer

**OR.1** The Reviewer accepts a recorded assignment under I11.4 and checks its freshness under I11.4 before reviewing.

**OR.2** The Reviewer checks requirement coverage, source provenance, decision ownership, provisional containment, justified holds, required assurance, and stewardship against actual evidence.

**OR.3** The Reviewer checks defaults, values, data shapes, error behavior, and identity handling for project properties specified outside authoritative direction or a valid C5.11 scope.

**OR.4** Findings distinguish demonstrated defects, unresolved project decisions, and optional suggestions.

**OR.5** The Reviewer reports technical disagreement exposing a missing property as a finding for the duty holder.

**OR.6** Review conclusions provide quality evidence, not acceptance, promotion, adoption, or authority to repair the reviewed work.
