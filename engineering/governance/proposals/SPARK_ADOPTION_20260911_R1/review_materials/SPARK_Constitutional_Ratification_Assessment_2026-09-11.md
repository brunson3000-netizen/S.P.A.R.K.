## Targeted framework corrections

### 4. Correct DEFERRED so postponement does not imply commitment

The DAI vocabulary defines DEFERRED as “Operator committed in principle and deferred implementation.” S.P.A.R.K. also deliberately leaves selections open, including transport/deployment. Treating all postponed decisions as commitments can foreclose an option; mapping FUTURE to DEFERRED can authorize unintended work.

Proposed replacement definition: “DEFERRED — The Operator postponed a decision or its implementation; the source states which and what, if anything, is committed.” Preserve source status and scope in the row. Do not mechanically translate FROZEN, PROVISIONAL, PROFILE DATA, PENDING CALIBRATION, FUTURE and OUT OF SCOPE into governance-adoption stages. Their original meanings remain in blueprint §2.3. The four universe governance stages are not automatically S.P.A.R.K. policy.

### 5. Put review timing in the operational policy home

A5.2 requires Operator-selected durations and replacement count inside the hashed governing manifest. Changing a timeout for a slow or hand-carried channel consequently changes an adopted governing member. This creates recurring ratification burden for a routine coordination parameter.

Proposed A5.2 replacement: “The duty holder records finite per-request and stage deadlines and a replacement limit in the mission or referenced channel policy, within Operator-approved resource limits. The stage deadline includes retries and replacements.”

Keep I3.13/I3.15 bounded stages, narrower applicable limits, no self-waiver and dependent-transition holds. Adjust configuration/readiness tooling to read the referenced operational record. Do not import 180/600 seconds from examples as an approved setting. Actual channel eligibility and economic/egress authority must be sourced; a provider name or NIM availability is insufficient. Retain the convergence protocol's Fable and NIM direction in its own home, not the Constitution.

### 6. Remove the requirement that the first mission be a new UUID mission

A2.3 says existing identifiers remain valid, but `framework.py ready()` requires `first_mission_id` to match a new MIS-UUID4. That prevents directly using an existing phase/gate identifier as the first mission even with a complete valid grant. It can be worked around by a wrapper, but adoption should not require re-identifying established work.

Proposed correction: resolve an existing source-bound mission by the registry; require UUID4 only for newly issued framework IDs. Keep mission/path consistency and required grant fields. Move the operational first-mission pointer outside the hashed governing configuration so it does not become stale constitutional identity. Add a legacy-mission readiness fixture. This is a migration/tooling correction, not a new permission.

### 7. Remove permanent retention of every old clause as a test requirement

`replay_checks.py` unconditionally asserts that all 258 identifiers from universal 1.0.0 remain present. Stable identifiers are valuable; mandatory perpetual presence is not equivalent to C9.4's authorized disposition. The test would reject a legitimate simplification deleting a superseded provision.

Replace that fixture with validation of the current declared clause inventory and explicit retired-clause dispositions. Never reuse a retired identifier for unrelated meaning. Keep duplicate-ID, link, accepted-content and drift checks.

### 8. Strengthen capture failure handling in I5.3

I5.3 covers capture before cross-session reliance or foreseeable session end. S.P.A.R.K.'s existing capture principle additionally requires surfacing inability to preserve material before reliance beyond the current exchange.

Proposed appended sentence: “If durable capture is unavailable, the agent reports the custody gap before relying on the material beyond the current exchange.” Preserve the existing project's capture timing and repository destination through the disposition map. Do not archive ordinary conversational exploration.
