# S.P.A.R.K. — Durable Conversation Capture Principle

**Status:** CANONICAL — OPERATOR-AUTHORIZED PROJECT PRINCIPLE — NON-ARCHITECTURAL
**Authority:** Direct Operator authorization
**Recorded:** 2026-09-06
**Scope:** Project-wide

## Principle

If something from conversation is materially worth future reliance, it must be durably captured in the S.P.A.R.K. repository before work advances far enough that it could be lost from conversation context.

## Repository custody

An agent with repository access is responsible for using that access to preserve material project memory within its granted authority. The Operator should not have to remember loose decisions, findings, evidence, task state, or research merely to prevent their loss.

Material worth future reliance includes, as applicable:

- Operator decisions and constraints;
- material research findings and evidence;
- experimental results and incident lessons;
- task/work state needed for later resumption;
- durable handoff state;
- open questions or deferred matters that later work will depend on.

Routine preservation may be filed as **WORKING / NON-OPERATIVE** material and does not require case-by-case Operator approval.

## Authority boundary

Preservation does not manufacture authority.

Repository presence alone does not promote working material, research, evidence, proposals, or candidates into canon. Architecture promotion, phase authorization, merge/deploy, or other separately reserved authority-changing effects still require the authorization applicable to that effect.

When the material being preserved is itself an explicit Operator decision already designated canonical, preserve it in the correct authoritative lane rather than downgrading it to working research.

## Failure to capture

If an agent cannot durably capture material that future work must rely on, it must surface that custody gap before relying on the material beyond the current exchange.
