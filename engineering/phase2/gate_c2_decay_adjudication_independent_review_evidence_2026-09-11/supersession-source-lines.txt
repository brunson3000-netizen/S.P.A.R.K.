250: ### Q7 — Decay/recovery: **rule-layer operations over existing cell fields; no encoding change**
251:
252: **Decision.** Decay and recovery are declarative rule operations (Q1 enum members), not
253: new cell machinery:
254:
255: - **Target/floor:** the existing `StateCell.baseline` field is the declared
256:   decay/recovery target. A decay/recovery rule references the target definition and its
257:   baseline; rules on definitions without a baseline semantics are rejected at rule-set
258:   activation.
259: - **Rate/cadence:** rule parameters, referencing `ConfigRevision` keys where hot-tunable
260:   (ADR-0004 change class 1).
261: - **Closed-form catch-up (frozen):** decay/recovery computes the new value as a closed-
262:   form function of (current value, baseline, rate, whole elapsed cadence steps between
263:   `updated_at` and now) in `i128` with the Q1 floor rule, clamped to declared bounds and
264:   the baseline — linear per-step; curved profiles compose the piecewise-curve operation.
265:   Catch-up over N missed steps in one evaluation is therefore bit-identical to N separate
266:   evaluations, so batching/chunking cannot alter outcomes (§28 item 2, §18.3
267:   deterministic catch-up).

116: ## 4. Vocabulary (binding)
117:
118: - **Request.** One external input: `Advance{T}` or `Command{request}` (§8 defines the
119:   command request's fields). Its **horizon** `h` is `T` or the command's `effective_time`.
120: - **Horizon.** An inclusive eligibility bound on which resident scheduled work may be
121:   consumed by the request. Never the canonical time of anything evaluated.
122: - **Canonical time of a cohort.** A scheduled cohort's `due_time`; a command cohort's
123:   `effective_time`. Every inherited use of `now`, `at`, and "current logical time" during
124:   that cohort's evaluation denotes this value. Delayed work created by a cohort carries
125:   `due_time ≥ canonical_time + 1` under checked `u64` addition; overflow rejects the wave
126:   atomically (R-3).
