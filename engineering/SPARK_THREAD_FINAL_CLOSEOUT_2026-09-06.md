## 8. Product-direction material worth preserving without premature freeze

### 8.1 Population-scaling intent

The operator's product intent is to maximize the number of globally persistent NPCs, monsters, and animals while keeping most actors deliberately lean.

Simulation sophistication should be heterogeneous rather than universal:

- ordinary/background actors may carry minimal state and simple behavior;
- regular citizens carry modest additional social/economic state;
- business owners, leaders, kings, and major named actors may justify richer state and causal processing.

A leaner variable ontology is desirable because it can increase simulation capacity and reduce causal fan-out. Earlier numeric NPC-capacity estimates in chat were speculative engineering estimates and are **not benchmark evidence**.

This intent should inform later performance/ontology work but does not modify the currently frozen Phase-0/1 contracts by itself.

### 8.2 Voice / TTS direction

The operator favored the architectural direction that S.P.A.R.K. should own **expression intent and voice-profile metadata**, while the host owns the actual TTS/speech-synthesis engine.

This is preserved as a future Phase-9 design direction, not promoted here as a newly frozen architecture contract. It should be reconciled against the controlling blueprint when Phase 9 is actually opened.

### 8.3 NIM semantic-assistance opportunity

A particularly promising NIM use case is design-time semantic/ontology work: naming variables and conditions, distinguishing overlapping concepts, finding umbrella terms, identifying redundant state, and proposing stable machine-readable vocabulary. NIM proposals remain advisory; canonical definitions remain under project authority.
